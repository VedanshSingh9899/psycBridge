psycBridge • for our business friend — start from zero

Private help.
Perfect doctor.

Uber-like access to psychiatrists and psychologists, with privacy so deep even family does not know if the user wants that.

Problem

  • People in India seek help late — severe, not mild.
  • Fear of family finding out stops the first call.
  • ID, OTP, long forms make a distressed person leave.
  • 5 senior doctors are overbooked. 500 young psychologists sit empty.
  • Patients repeat their trauma story to every new doctor.

Solution in one line

One tap to talk. No login. First minutes free.
File moves doctor to doctor till perfect fit. Patient stays with one owner.

Direct money to doctors at start. Small fee only after both sides love us.

For users

  • Open app → Talk Now → auto nickname → human in minutes.
  • 8-10 min free chat or audio. No video pressure.
  • Follow-up only if helpful. Neutral reminders, neutral billing.
  • First chat gives 2 days relief. Sessions plus medicine give recovery.

For doctors — care graph

  • Each doctor keeps 5-10 trusted peers across fields.
  • If a friend fits better, send brief. Other replies Yes / No / Try C.
  • Rule: who holds the file, owns the patient till next yes.
  • Off-app referral allowed. Juniors get warm cases, seniors reduce overload.

Who does what

Tech — you

  • Build Talk Now MVP: guest session, queue, in-app chat audio
  • Brief share with consent and expiry
  • Privacy basics: alias, neutral alerts

Learn: WebRTC basics, DPDP privacy, Telepsychiatry rules overview

Psych friend

  • Define 60-sec brief format
  • Recruit 5-6 volunteers, supervise quality
  • Safety and escalation protocol
  • Decide what data docs actually need

Learn: product thinking, training peers, triage design

Business friend

  • Validate chanda then fee model, costs
  • Onboard 2 psychiatrists + 3 psychologists
  • College pilot: 50 users, Talk Hours 7-11pm
  • Legal entity, consent templates

Learn: mental health market, MHCA 2017, unit economics of free-first

What we all learn together

  • Privacy is the product, not a setting.
  • Crisis is not Uber — partner helpline, break-glass consent.
  • Trust over lock-in: doctors can leave anytime.
